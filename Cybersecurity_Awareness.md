**CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS GUIDE**
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting networks, computers, systems and personal information from cyber threats.
**A) Password Security**
- Use strong passwords
- Change passwords if you suspect they have been compromised
- Avoid sharing your passwords with others
**B) Phishing Awareness**
Phishing is a fraudulent email sent by attackers with the aim of tricking people into revealing their personal information.
**Ways to prevent Phishing**
- Avoid clicking suspicious emails
- using email filters to identify fake emails
- Avoid sharing personal information through suspicious messages
**C)Ransomware Awareness**
Ransom is a form of malicious malware that encrypt and lock the systems information in demand for ransom for it to be recovered
**Ways to prevent Ransomware**
- Providing security awareness to employees in organisations
- Regular updates of systems to detect unusual networks on the system
- Installing firewalls to block and detect unusual network on the system
